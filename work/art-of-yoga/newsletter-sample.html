This Month's Practice Tip

2-Minute Reset

Before you even get out of bed tomorrow: reach your arms overhead and your toes toward the foot of the bed for 5 slow breaths, then hug both knees to your chest and rock gently side to side. That's it. Full breakdown (plus 3 more) is in this month's Journal post.
